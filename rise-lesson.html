    Welcome

    Some claims cannot be finalised during first review. Guidance may call for an additional review before processing can continue, and the guidance article tells you which review team receives the claim: Quality Review (QR) or Compliance Review (CR).

    In this lesson you will learn how to prepare a claim for additional review by following the guidance in the applicable hold article. You will explore the information that must be documented before routing and the actions used to send the claim to the right team.

    After completing this lesson, you will be able to:

    • Decide whether a held claim goes to QR or CR, based on hold guidance.
    • Identify the review packet information to complete before routing.
    • Apply the correct routing action once the claim is ready.

    You will also complete a guided walkthrough in a practice version of ClaimDesk, a fictional claims tool used for training.

    Part 6 of 6

    Summary: The Right Route Starts with the Right Preparation

    You have just completed Routing Held Claims for Additional Review. You learned how hold guidance directs claims to specialised review paths when additional evaluation is required before processing can continue. Rather than choosing the destination independently, processors rely on the applicable hold article to decide whether a claim goes to QR or CR.

    Key takeaways

    • Follow the hold guidance. The applicable article determines whether review is required and names the path. Always read it before preparing a claim for routing.
    • Prepare the claim for review. Place the hold, complete line guidance and document related claims where applicable.
    • Route with the right action. Use the routing action that matches the review path, once the packet is complete.
